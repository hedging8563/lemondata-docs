## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Instance">
    Go to your [LemonData Dashboard](https://lemondata.cc/dashboard/claw) and click **Create Instance**. Choose a subdomain — your instance will be available at `claw-{subdomain}.lemondata.cc`. The Dashboard is the control plane for lifecycle actions such as create, renew, rebuild, restart, and upgrade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish Runtime Setup in WebUI">
    Open the **WebUI** at `claw-{subdomain}.lemondata.cc` to configure which models are available, connect IM channels, tune trigger behavior, and manage skills and governance settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a Chat Platform">
    In the WebUI, configure one or more messaging platforms. Most platforms take under 5 minutes to set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Send a message to your bot — it will automatically pair with you and start responding with AI!
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Auto-Pairing Works

Most platforms (Telegram, Discord, Slack, Feishu) use an **auto-pairing** mechanism:

1. Send your bot a message on any connected platform
2. The **first user** per channel is automatically paired as the **owner** — no code needed!
3. Additional users will receive a **pairing code** to enter in the Claw WebUI at `claw-{subdomain}.lemondata.cc`

<Note>
**WhatsApp** uses QR code scanning instead of pairing. **WeCom** uses webhook callbacks and does not require pairing. **DingTalk** and **QQ** currently have platform-specific limitations for group delivery and should be treated as capability-scoped integrations rather than full auto-pairing parity.
</Note>

<Warning>
By default, only paired users can interact with your bot. You can change this in the DM Policy settings, but be careful with the "open" policy as it allows anyone to use your bot (and your AI credits).
</Warning>
