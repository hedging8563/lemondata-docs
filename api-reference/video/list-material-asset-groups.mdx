---
title: "List Material Asset Groups"
openapi: "GET /v1/videos/assets/groups"
description: "Lists Seedance material groups owned by the current organization."
---

Lists material groups owned by the current organization. Filter by `library_type` to separate `aigc_avatar` virtual-avatar groups from `liveness_face` real-person groups.

Real-person groups returned here were created by the verification bind flow. Use their TokenLab `id` as `group_id` when uploading real-person material assets.
