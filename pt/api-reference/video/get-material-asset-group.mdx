---
title: "Get Material Asset Group"
openapi: "GET /v1/videos/assets/groups/{groupId}"
description: "Retrieves one Seedance material group by TokenLab group ID."
---

Retrieves one organization-owned material group. The response includes `library_type`, display metadata, and group status.

For real-person groups, keep the returned TokenLab `id` as the stable public group identifier. TokenLab does not expose underlying material-library IDs in this response.
