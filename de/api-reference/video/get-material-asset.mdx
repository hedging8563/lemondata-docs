---
title: "Get Material Asset"
openapi: "GET /v1/videos/assets/{assetId}"
description: "Retrieves one Seedance material asset by TokenLab material asset ID."
---

Retrieves one material asset and its ingestion status.

Status values are `PROCESSING`, `ACTIVE`, `FAILED`, and `DELETED`. Use the asset in video generation only after `status` becomes `ACTIVE`. If `FAILED`, inspect `error_message`, fix the source material, and create a new asset.
